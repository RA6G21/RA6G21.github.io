About Me & Contact

I like understanding why things work and why they sometimes don’t.

I’m a Mechatronics & Robotics Engineering MSc graduate from the University of Leeds, following a BEng in Aerospace Engineering at Southampton. My strongest interests are mechanical design, robotics, actuation, sensing and control systems.

I enjoy switching between design, simulation, fabrication and testing. Building parts and working with engineering teams are as interesting to me as the modelling behind them.

Outside engineering, football is important to me. I captained a team during my undergraduate years, and I enjoy the combination of teamwork, responsibility and competition.

Technical interests

Mechanical Design, Robotics, Aerospace & Control Systems

I'm interested in engineering projects that combine mechanical design, analysis and control. I enjoy designing mechanisms, understanding how systems behave, and working through the challenges of turning an ideainto something that can be tested. My background is in both aerospace and mechatronics, so I'm particularly interested in work that brings these areas together.

Some examples of my work:

  • Robotics & Control: Developed and compared PID, LQR and LQI controllers for a jumping self-balancing wheeled-biped robot, including navigation, obstacle avoidance and jumping in MuJoCo.
  • Mechanical Design & Manufacturing: Worked on robotic mechanisms, CAD design, laser cutting, carbon-fibre layup, assembly and structural analysis through Eurobot and Gryphon Arrows.
  • Aerodynamics & Simulation: Used ANSYS Fluent to investigate VTOL motor-mount configurations, achieving a 15.87% drag reduction in the evaluated configuration.

I'd like to keep developing my experience in designing, buildingand testing mechanical and mechatronic systems, whether that's inrobotics, aerospace or another engineering field.
