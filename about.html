About Me & Contact

I'm interested in robotics and mechanical design, especially projects where I can actually build and test something.

I studied Aerospace Engineering at Southampton, then moved into Mechatronics and Robotics for my MSc at Leeds. Along the way I've worked on quite different things, including an autonomous competition robot, a UAV with a manipulator, and a jumping wheeled-biped robot in MuJoCo.

I enjoy the process of working through a design, testing it and trying to understand why something doesn't work as expected. I particularly want to keep developing my hands-on design and manufacturing experience.

Outside engineering I enjoy football. I was vice-captain of River Plato from 2022 to 2023, where I gave speeches and took part in organising team events. That gave me plenty of experience speaking in front of people and working with a team outside the classroom.

Technical interests

Design: Onshape, SOLIDWORKS (CSWA), Fusion 360, mechanisms and drawings.
Robotics: Python, MuJoCo, MATLAB/Simulink, feedback control and path planning.
Analysis: ANSYS Fluent, CFD, FEA and validation.
Hardware: 3D printing, laser cutting, assembly, sensors, LiDAR and motors/servos.
