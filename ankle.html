Biomechatronics · CAD & FEA

Ankle rehabilitation exoskeleton

An ankle-assist mechanism I designed using a bidirectional winch concept.

The design

I designed a mechanism intended to allow pitch, roll and yaw motion at the ankle, using a bidirectional winch for actuation.

What I checked

  • Modelled the parts and how they fit together in CAD.
  • Used FEA to look at stress and displacement under loading.
  • Found a highly loaded area around the tendon attachment and looked at ways to reinforce it.

This work is a design and analysis study, not a claim of physical clinical validation.
